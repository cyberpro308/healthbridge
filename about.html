About HealthBridge

Bridging the gap between Nigerian communities and world-class medical care since 2008.

Our Story

Compassionate Care Rooted in Community

HealthBridge Medical Center was founded in 2008 with a simple but ambitious vision: to make world-class healthcare accessible to every Nigerian family. What began as a modest clinic in Victoria Island has grown into a leading multi-specialty medical center trusted by tens of thousands of patients each year.

Today, our team of over 50 specialists combines advanced technology with a deeply human approach — because we believe great medicine treats the person, not just the condition.

  • ✓ Accredited by the Nigerian Medical & Dental Council
  • ✓ Internationally trained specialists
  • ✓ State-of-the-art diagnostic laboratories

Our Mission
